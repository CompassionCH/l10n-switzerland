## 18.0.1.0.1

- Wizard pre-filled: last year, and the salary rules used the last time;
  it opens the declarations of the year once generated.
- Other lump-sum expenses (13.2.3, e.g. home office allowances): salary
  rules and kind chosen in the wizard.
- Standard remark "child allowances paid by the compensation fund"
  (field 15) as an option of the wizard.
- Employee particulars (language, names, address) read from the
  employee's private address fields: `address_home_id` no longer exists
  since 17.0 and the XML could not be generated.
